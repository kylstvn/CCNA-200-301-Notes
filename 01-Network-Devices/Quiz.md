# QUIZ

### Question 1

#### Your company wants to purchase some network hardware to which they can plug the 30 PCs in your department. Which type of network is appropriate?
- a router
- a firewall
- a switch
- a server

#### Answer: a switch

#### Wrong Answer: 
- a router 
    A router, like this Cisco ISR 900 series router, is designed for forwarding traffic between networks, not for connecting lots of end hosts like PCs to. 
    A router will not typically have 30 network interfaces to connect hosts to.
